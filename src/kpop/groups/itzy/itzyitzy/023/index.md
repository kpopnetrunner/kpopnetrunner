This episode shows Ryujin preparing for her birthday. She created birthday invitations and placed them in each of the other Itzy member's lockers. Ryujin had the first birthday since Itzy debuted. Chaeryeong, Yeji, and Yuna go to buy a birthday cake from a bakery across the street from the JYP building. Chaeryeoung, Yeji, Yuna, and Lia then decorate for Ryujin's birthday party. I liked how everyone was so happy celebrating Ryujin's birthday.
